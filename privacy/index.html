Privacy Policy

Effective: July 2, 2026 · Last updated: July 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how eTherm Solutions LLC ("TrueLoad," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the TrueLoad web application at app.trueloadapp.com and related services (collectively, the "Service").

TrueLoad is a business-to-business (B2B) service used by licensed electrical contractors and professionals. This Policy covers information about you as a registered user ("Account Holder") as well as information about your customers ("End Customers") that you enter into the Service in the course of performing load calculations.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you are entering information about an End Customer, you represent that you have obtained all necessary consents and authorizations from that customer to share their information with TrueLoad as described herein.

TrueLoad acts in two roles, depending on the information involved. For your account information and the data you provide about yourself, TrueLoad decides how and why that information is used and acts as the controller. For information you enter about an End Customer, including AMI interval data, you decide the purpose of the processing, and TrueLoad processes that information on your behalf and under your direction as a processor. In that role TrueLoad uses End Customer information only to provide the Service to you.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

When you register for a TrueLoad account, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Company name and business address
  • Electrical contractor license number (if provided)
  • Password (stored in hashed, non-recoverable form via Supabase Auth)

2.2 Project and Calculation Data

When you use the Service to perform load calculations, we collect the data you enter, including:

  • End Customer name and service address
  • Electrical service details (service rating, voltage, utility provider)
  • Proposed equipment list (equipment type, nameplate ratings, quantities)
  • AMI interval data files uploaded to the Service (see Section 5 for special handling)
  • Calculation inputs, outputs, and project status

2.3 Payment Information

Payment transactions are processed by Stripe, Inc. TrueLoad does not collect or store your full credit card number, CVV, or bank account details. We receive from Stripe: the last four digits of your payment card, card brand, and transaction confirmation identifiers. Stripe's privacy practices are governed by Stripe's own Privacy Policy at stripe.com/privacy.

2.4 Usage and Technical Data

When you use the Service, we and our infrastructure providers may collect technical and usage information, including:

  • IP address and general location derived from it
  • Browser type and version, and operating system
  • Pages and features used, actions taken, and time spent in the Service
  • Error logs and diagnostic data

We use this information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Service, to understand how it is used, and to improve its features, accuracy, reliability, and performance. We do not use it to advertise to you, and we do not sell it, share it, or track you across other websites.

2.5 AMI Authorization Consent Records

When you initiate an AMI data authorization request on behalf of an End Customer, we log:

  • The End Customer's email address (to send the authorization request)
  • The IP address and timestamp at which the End Customer provided authorization
  • The utility to which the data request was directed
  • The text of the authorization the End Customer agreed to, so the record reflects what they actually approved

This log serves as the authorization record and may be included in or referenced by reports generated by the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your identity
  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service and its calculation features
  • Process payments and manage billing
  • Send transactional emails, including account confirmation, password reset, AMI authorization requests, and utility data request emails
  • Respond to support requests and communications
  • Monitor for unauthorized use, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Analyze usage and technical data, including in aggregated or de-identified form, to improve our algorithms, parser accuracy, calculation methodology, and Service performance

We do not sell your personal information or your End Customers' information to third parties. We do not use your project data for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with the following service providers as necessary to operate the Service. Each provider is bound by contractual obligations to protect the information:

Supabase — Database and authentication infrastructure. Stores all account, project, and AMI data. Data is stored on servers in the United States. supabase.com/privacy

Stripe, Inc. — Payment processing. Receives payment card details directly from your browser; TrueLoad does not pass raw card data to Stripe. stripe.com/privacy

Resend — Transactional email delivery. Processes email addresses for sending account confirmation, password reset, AMI authorization, and utility request emails. resend.com/privacy

Automated data verification. We use Anthropic, an artificial intelligence service provider, to perform automated quality verification of uploaded utility meter data. Data shared for this purpose is limited to de-identified interval readings and file structure information; customer names, service addresses, and account identifiers are removed before transmission.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms that track users across websites, or data brokers.

5. AMI Interval Data — Special Handling

AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure) interval data is electric utility meter data that records a customer's electricity consumption at regular intervals (typically 15 minutes). This data can reveal patterns of activity within a home or business and is therefore treated with heightened care.

5.1 Purpose Limitation

AMI data uploaded to TrueLoad is used exclusively to perform NEC 220.87 load calculations for the specific project for which it was uploaded. We do not aggregate AMI data across customers for commercial purposes, sell AMI data to third parties, or use AMI data to infer lifestyle patterns for any purpose other than performing the requested calculation.

5.2 Account Holder Responsibility

You, as the Account Holder, are responsible for obtaining valid End Customer authorization before uploading any AMI data to the Service. By uploading AMI data, you represent that the End Customer has authorized you to access and share their utility interval data for the purpose of this load calculation.

5.3 Retention

AMI interval data is retained in association with your project as long as your account is active and for up to 30 days after account termination to allow data export. You may delete AMI data from a project at any time within the Service.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information and project data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as transaction records for financial compliance). Aggregated, de-identified data derived from Service usage may be retained indefinitely. TrueLoad maintains this data in de-identified form, does not attempt to re-identify it, and commits to keeping it de-identified.

Transaction records (charges, subscription history) are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years in accordance with financial record-keeping requirements.

7. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Passwords stored using industry-standard hashing (never stored in plaintext)
  • Role-based access controls limiting which personnel can access user data
  • Authentication required for functions that access Account Holder or project data; functions an End Customer must reach without a TrueLoad account (such as the AMI authorization page) are protected instead by unique, expiring access tokens

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, hacking, data loss, or other breaches will never occur. If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you without unreasonable delay and as required by applicable state breach notification law.
