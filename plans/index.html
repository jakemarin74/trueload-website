Enterprise

Built for firms, not just individuals

Enterprise gives a firm one account that many people can work from — with the billing, visibility, and administration a growing team actually needs. Pricing and seat count are configured around how your firm operates, not a fixed tier.

Multi-user seats

Add every technician, estimator, or office admin who needs to run a calculation, under one firm account. Seats can be added or removed as your team changes.

Centralized billing

One invoice for the whole firm instead of individual cards on file. No reconciling per-user charges or chasing reimbursements.

Firm-level project visibility

Admins see every project the firm has run, not just their own — useful for QA, tracking job status across crews, and keeping records in one place.

Volume pricing

Per-seat cost is negotiated based on team size and expected usage, so a five-person crew and a fifty-person firm aren't priced the same way.

Admin controls

A designated firm administrator manages who has access, independent of any single person's individual login.

Priority support & onboarding

Direct help getting a team set up, plus priority response when questions come up during a job.
